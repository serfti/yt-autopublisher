Privacy Policy — Clips Tom Uploader

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Clips Tom Uploader ("the App") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App accesses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's Google account (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • Basic channel information (channel title, subscriber and video counts) to confirm the correct channel.
  • Metadata of videos the owner uploads (title, description, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time).

How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel and to schedule their publication. The App does not access other users' data, does not run ads, and does not analyze or sell any data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube APIs. No data is shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner deletes the local token file or revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account security settings (Third-party apps with account access). After revocation the App can no longer access the account.

Cookies

The App does not use cookies and does not store information on any user's device other than the owner's local token file.
