YT Autopublisher

YT Autopublisher is a private, single-user tool that uploads short gaming videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Clips Tom, and schedules their publication time.

What it does

  • Signs in to the owner's YouTube channel with Google OAuth.
  • Uploads finished vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) prepared by the owner.
  • Sets title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time.

The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, and only for the owner's own channel.
